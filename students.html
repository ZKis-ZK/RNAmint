Student opportunities

Opportunities and student research activity across RNA manufacturing, modelling and process development.

Our brilliant PhD students make Sheffield what it is – a research powerhouse, a multidisciplinary community, a place where ideas matter. We want you to bring your ideas and your enthusiasm for your chosen discipline so please contact us and state your interests and expertise.

Current PhD researchers

Shady Hamed — PhD Researcher (01/2025 - 01/2028)

2nd Supervisor - Prof Joan Cordiner

Shady is currently pursuing his PhD in the School of Chemical, Materials and Biological Engineering at the University of Sheffield. Shady’s research focuses on modeling mRNA vaccine production process units involving purification and drug product formulation: Chromatography, LNP formulation, and Tangential flow filtration. Shady is passionate about contributing to the mRNA vaccine project, which aims to push the boundaries of human knowledge forward and work towards eradicating more diseases.

Contact Shady via LinkedIn or email s.m.hamed@sheffield.ac.uk

Haitem Elmassoudi — PhD Researcher (09/2025 - 03/2029)
