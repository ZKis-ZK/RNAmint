RNA Manufacturing Innovation Team (RNA MINT)

Innovating and digitalising RNA production processes.

We combine experimental research and computational modelling to accelerate the development and manufacture of high-quality, low-cost RNA vaccines and therapeutics to combat a wide range of diseases.
